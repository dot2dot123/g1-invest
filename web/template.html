G1 Score (0–100): the validated quant score, a percentile among the top 1,000 US stocks. Since v4.2 it blends four slow pillars equally: Buffett & Munger quality, lean Magic 15, cyclically-adjusted value (5-year median earnings yield and FCF yield vs sector peers) and trend & defense (12-month momentum vs sector, low downside beta). Weak-quality names (Buffett & Munger below 4/10) are capped at 65. It passed a pre-registered test against the older composite.

Horizon strip: under every score, 3m · 6m · 1y · 2y · 3y · 4y · 5y. G1 6m–5y are the historical odds of beating the S&P 500 at that horizon for similar-score stocks (0–100). G1 3m is the same idea from a pre-registered 3-month test of v4.2 vs the older composite (the winner is shown; marked unvalidated if its rank-IC t is below 2). The 3m slot also shows Danelfin’s AI Score (1–10, their 3-month view) when the uploaded file has one; otherwise n/a. Those two 3m scores are not blended. Model estimates, not advice.

Dossier pill: a separate, unvalidated read from one web search per company (−10…+10): moat, hidden stakes, AI threats, cyclical-peak penalty, pricing power. It never changes the G1 Score.

Upside / Safety: percentiles (0–100) among the top 1,000 US stocks: growth, trend and value vs low tail risk, balance sheet and quality. The verdict rules use them.

Verdicts: STRONG BUY strong score (75+), Upside 70+, Safety 60+ and price above its 40-week average. ACCUMULATE same, but up 15%+ in a month: buy half now. WAIT FOR TREND strong business in a pullback: wait for the 10-week EMA or the Deep-Value price. CLIMAX TRIM only with your Entry $: up 40%+, stretched 35% above the 40-week average on a huge-volume week that closed weak. SELL / BROKEN below the 40-week average and weak (score under 45, or falling margins with a score under 70). HOLD everything else.

1-yr odds: how often similar-scoring stocks beat the S&P 500 over 12 months, shown against the usual rate, plus Bear · Base · Bull prices. Size: edge over the usual rate ÷ downside volatility → Starter 1–2%, Core 3–5%, High conviction 6–8%; halved while waiting for trend or within 7 days of earnings.

Stops: without an Entry $, an initial risk stop plus buy triggers. Type your Entry $ in a holding's details (saved only in this browser) to switch on breakeven at +20% and a 10-week EMA trail at +40%.

Cash plan: stocks share = 0.14 ÷ the S&P 500's 1-year downside volatility, between 50% and 100%; the rest in T-bills (SGOV). Danelfin (owner's page only) comes from uploaded CSVs.

Method, data and limitations

Live score. G1 v4.2 (docs/PREREGISTRATION_V4_2.md) was pre-registered before any result, has no fitted parameters, and beat the v2 composite's rank IC at all six horizons with lower turnover. Names without SEC fundamentals (ETFs, some foreign or newly listed companies) use a price-only score.

No look-ahead. Fundamentals come from SEC XBRL (first-filed values, usable 2 days after filing). Regime inputs (S&P 500 vs its 40-week average; high-yield spread from FRED, free history since Nov 2023) are taken as of each date.

Survivorship. The free-data universe holds only companies still trading, so results are flattered and the delisting haircut never triggered. Long horizons rest on few independent periods.

Execution rules. Verdicts, stops, sizes and the cash plan are simple, transparent UI rules written down in advance; they were not backtested.

Business dossiers. One free web search per company plus an analyst-style JSON judgment, cached 30 days, every claim linked to a source. They penalize cyclical-peak earnings (e.g. memory chips, commodities, shippers at record margins), reward hidden stakes (e.g. Waymo, Anthropic) and flag AI/agent threats. Not point-in-time; tracked live in a weekly audit ledger.

Refresh. Pulls 5 years of weekly prices per name in your browser: G1's own proxy if configured, otherwise Yahoo directly, then free CORS proxies (corsproxy.io, codetabs, AllOrigins), each with a timeout. Nothing runs on a schedule.
