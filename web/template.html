How to read this
G1 Score (0–100): where the stock ranks among the top 1,000 US stocks on G1's validated live score (today: the v2 composite of quality, value, low dilution and balance sheet). "v4*" adds the business-dossier overlay (S_v4 = score + 1.5 × dossier points), which includes unvalidated analyst-style judgment.
Upside 🚀 / Safety 🛡️: v4 axes. Upside mixes growth, trend and value; Safety mixes tail risk, balance sheet and quality. They failed their pre-registered test against the composite, so treat them as descriptive.
Action verdict: STRONG BUY (top 20%), ACCUMULATE (Staggered) (top 20% but up more than 15% in a month: buy half now), HOLD, CLIMAX TRIM (more than 50% above the 40-week average after a 15%+ month), SELL / BROKEN (bottom 20%, or below the 40-week average and 30% off the high).
1-yr win odds & $ range: how often similar-scoring stocks beat the S&P 500 over 12 months out of sample, and Bear · Base · Bull dollar targets from the same backtest (10th / 50th / 90th percentile, assuming a flat S&P 500).
Size: half-Kelly from those odds and ranges, scaled down for high downside volatility, max 10%, halved when results are expected within 7 days. Stops ratchet: the 40-week average or a typical bad week, then breakeven after +20%, then a 10-week EMA trail after +40%.
Danelfin (owner's pages only): sub-scores from the uploaded portfolio CSVs; Agree / Disagree compares them with G1's top/bottom 20%.

Method, data and limitations

Live score. G1 v4 tested a five-sleeve engine (quality, value vs adjusted EV, growth and inflection, trend and volatility coil, left-tail shield) blended by market regime (S_quant), against the v2 composite. The rules were committed before the test (docs/PREREGISTRATION_V4.md). S_quant did not beat the composite, so the v2 composite stays the live score. Upside and Safety are shown for context only. Names without SEC fundamentals (ETFs, some foreign or multi-class filers) use the price-only score (momentum, drawdown, volatility contraction, downside beta and tail loss).

No look-ahead. Fundamentals come from SEC XBRL companyfacts, using the first-filed value for each period, usable 2 days after filing. Regime inputs (S&P 500 vs its 40-week average; ICE BofA high-yield spread from FRED, free history since Nov 2023) are taken as of each date. Insider-buying clusters come from SEC's free Form 4 data sets.

Survivorship. The free-data universe holds only companies still trading today, so the −30% delisting haircut never triggered in the test, and results are flattered by survivorship. Most of the edge comes from 2020–2022. The 36–60 month results rest on very few independent periods.

Market Weather. Sunny = S&P 500 above its 40-week average and the high-yield spread below 4.5%. Storm Warning = below the average or spread above 5.5%. Cloudy = in between. Storm: preserve cash = below the average with fewer than 35% of large stocks above theirs; the page then suggests a 30–50% T-bill reserve and pauses new buys. "Clearing after the storm" (CAPITULATION BUY) needs a 15%+ drawdown, breadth recovering above 40% and spreads easing.

Business dossiers. Built for the books and the G1 Top 30 from SEC filings data (no paid data, no LLM), cached 14 days. Five rule-based scores: moat vs disruption, hidden stakes, talent and R&D, pricing power and backlog, and variant perception gap. Each comes with source links, and thin evidence scores 0. They are not point-in-time and cannot be backtested.

Refresh. Pulls 5 years of weekly prices per name from Yahoo in your browser (AllOrigins proxy if Yahoo blocks it) and recomputes price-based features and valuation with the bundled fundamentals. Volatility-contraction and up/down-volume inputs use daily data as of the build. Nothing runs on a schedule.
